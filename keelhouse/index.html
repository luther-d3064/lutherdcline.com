The problem

You run a lot. Nobody runs the background for you.

Most capable people carry an invisible second job: the inbox, the follow-ups, the forms, the bills, the appointments, the people they keep meaning to call. It doesn't show up on a calendar, and it never ends.

Too many loose threads

Commitments live in email, texts, notes, and your head. Things slip, not because you don't care, but because nothing is holding them for you.

AI tools that don't add up

You've tried chatbots and apps. Each one helps a little, none of them talk to each other, and none of them know you.

No safe way to delegate

Handing your life to software feels risky. Who sees your data? What can it send or spend? Without clear guardrails, it's easier to just do it yourself.

Your AI team

A chief of staff, a coordinating intelligence, and a bench of specialists.

We install a personal AI team that you own. Your AI chief of staff orchestrates the day. Astra AI is the coordinating intelligence behind it, planning the work, directing each specialist, and keeping the whole team in sync. Each role has a clear job and clear limits.

Astra AI Coordinating intelligence

Plans the week, assigns work to the right specialist, resolves overlaps, and keeps everyone working from the same picture. You never have to manage the bots. Astra does.

Chief of Staff Your front door

Your daily briefing, routing of requests to the right specialist, and relentless follow-up on open loops. One place to ask, one place to approve.

Paperwork & Legal Organizer

Tracks documents, deadlines, renewals, and forms. Summarizes what a document says, flags what needs attention, and tells you when it's time to call your attorney.

Money Tracker

Read-only view of your finances. Tracks budgets, recurring charges, and debt paydown progress, and flags anything unusual. It can't move money, and it refers real decisions to your financial professional.

Health & Habit Coach

Reads your wearable data, keeps your habits visible, and nudges gently. It notices trends and suggests adjustments, and it points you to your doctor for anything medical.

Career Advisor

Keeps your goals, wins, and network in view. Helps prep for reviews, conversations, and opportunities, so your next move is deliberate instead of reactive.

Faith & Values Companion Optional

A chaplain-style space for reflection, gratitude, and checking decisions against what you care about. Entirely opt-in, shaped around your own tradition or none at all.

Security Officer

Watches who and what has access to your accounts, flags unusual activity, and reviews new connections before they're trusted. Oversight is built in, not bolted on.

Relationships & Friendship

Remembers the people who matter: birthdays, the last time you talked, what's going on in their lives. Prompts the check-in. The call is still yours to make.

Docs & Research

Drafts documents, digs into questions, and brings back clear, sourced summaries, so you start from a first draft instead of a blank page.

Organizer and advisor roles summarize, flag, and refer. They do not provide legal, financial, medical, or other licensed professional advice.

A shared memory “brain”

Every role works from one private, organized memory of your priorities, people, projects, and preferences. Tell it once and the whole team knows.

Governance & trust

Powerful help. Firm guardrails. You stay in command.

Plenty of people can wire up AI. What makes Keelhouse different is governance. Your team is designed from day one to act on your behalf only within lines you draw, and to show its work.

The AI does the legwork. You make the calls.

  • Human in the loop

    Anything consequential waits for you. The team prepares; you approve.

  • No money moves without you

    Financial access is read-only. The team cannot pay, transfer, or trade.

  • Every spend needs your itemized yes

    Any proposed cost is laid out line by line, and nothing is purchased until you approve each item.

  • Drafts first, always

    Emails and messages are drafted for your review. Nothing goes out in your name unless you send it.

  • Full audit trail

    Every action, draft, and recommendation is logged, so you can see what happened, when, and why.

  • Security oversight

    A dedicated security role monitors access and flags anything unusual, with least-privilege access throughout.

  • You own everything

    All accounts are in your name and all data is yours. If we part ways, your team stays with you.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Who is this for?

Founders, executives, and busy professionals who want real leverage from AI without giving up control. If you have more to track than hours to track it, this is for you.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We handle the setup. Day to day, you read a briefing and approve or decline what the team proposes, in plain language.

Can the AI send emails or spend money on its own?

No. Messages are drafted for your approval first. Financial access is read-only, and any proposed spend needs your itemized yes before anything happens.

Who owns the accounts and data?

You do. Everything is set up in accounts in your name. If we stop working together, your team and your data stay with you.

Is this legal, financial, or medical advice?

No. The organizer and advisor roles summarize, flag, and help you prepare. For decisions that need a licensed professional, they point you to one.

What tools does it run on?

Mainstream, well-established platforms you can see and control, connected only where you approve. We focus on the outcome and the guardrails, and we walk you through exactly what's connected during your install.

How long does setup take?

About 10 days from kickoff to a working team, followed by a tuning period as the team learns how you like things done.

Is the Faith & Values companion required?

Not at all. It's optional, and you choose whether to include it and how it should reflect your own beliefs.

What does pilot pricing mean?

We're taking on a small number of early clients at a reduced install rate in exchange for candid feedback. Ask on your discovery call.

Ready to hand off the background?

Start with a short, no-pressure discovery call. We'll look at where a governed AI team would help most, and whether it's a fit.
