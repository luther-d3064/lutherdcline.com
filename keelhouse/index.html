The problem

You run a lot. Nobody runs the background for you.

Most capable people carry an invisible second job: the inbox, the follow-ups, the forms, the appointments, the people they keep meaning to call. It doesn't show up on a calendar, and it never ends.

Too many loose threads

Commitments live in email, texts, notes, and your head. Things slip, not because you don't care, but because nothing is holding them for you.

AI tools that don't add up

You've tried chats and apps. Each one helps a little, none of them talk to each other, and none of them know you on Tuesday the way they did on Monday.

No safe way to delegate

Handing your email or your files to software feels risky. Who sees your data? What can it send or spend? Without clear guardrails, it's easier to just do it yourself.

What you get

A chief of staff, a clear set of rules, and routines that actually run.

We start with one named assistant that has a job: brief the morning, draft the follow-ups, remember the people and the rules, and escalate anything that needs you. Specialists can join later — money, research, paperwork, career, health, values — each with a clear lane and clear limits.

Chief of Staff Your front door

Your daily briefing, routing of requests, and relentless follow-up on open loops. One place to ask, one place to approve. You never have to manage a pile of chats.

Standing rules Built in from day one

Read-only first. Drafts first, always. No money moves without you. Private data stays private, and no passwords belong in chat. The safety layer is the work, not an add-on.

Governance & trust

Powerful help. Firm guardrails. You stay in command.

Plenty of people can wire up AI. What makes this work different is governance. The assistant is designed from day one to act only inside lines you draw, and to show its work.

The AI does the legwork. You make the calls.

  • Read-only first

    Email, calendar, and notes are connected so the assistant can see. Write access is earned one task at a time.

  • Drafts first, always

    Emails and messages are written for your review. Nothing goes out in your name unless you send it.

  • No money moves without you

    Finance, when you want it, stays read-only. The assistant cannot pay, transfer, or trade.

  • Every spend needs your itemized yes

    If anything would cost money, you see it line by line before it happens.

  • Private data stays private

    No passwords in chat. No copying your email or files into someone else's account.

  • Full audit trail

    Actions, drafts, and recommendations are logged so you can see what happened, when, and why.

  • You own everything

    You connect your own tools on your own screen. If we part ways, your setup stays with you.

  • Access is removed at handoff

    On Done-For-You work, access is removed at handoff. You confirm it in writing.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Who is this for?

Owners and solo operators — consultants, local services, ministry and nonprofit leaders, and other busy professionals — who want real help from AI without giving up control. If you have more to track than hours to track it, this is for you.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Day to day, you read a short brief and approve or decline what the assistant drafts, in plain language.

Can the AI send emails or spend money on its own?

No. Messages are drafted for your approval first. Financial access, when you use it, is read-only, and any proposed spend needs your itemized yes.

Who owns the accounts and data?

You do. Everything is set up in accounts in your name. If we stop working together, your setup and your data stay with you.

Is this legal, financial, medical, or pastoral advice?

No. The assistant helps you organize, summarize, and prepare. For decisions that need a licensed professional, you take it to one. Organizer and advisor roles do not provide legal, financial, medical, tax, or pastoral advice.

What tools does it run on?

Mainstream tools you can see and control — typically an AI assistant, email and calendar, and Notion or Google Drive — connected only where you approve. We walk through exactly what's connected before anything is turned on.

How do I start?

Most people start with a free 15-minute fit call, then the $100 group Quickstart. If you already know you want a playbook, a monthly working relationship, or a full install, say so on the call.

Is there a payment link on this page?

Is the Faith & Values companion required?

Not at all. It's optional, and you choose whether to include it and how it should reflect your own beliefs.
